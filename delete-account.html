How to ask for your account to be deleted
  1. Email asccur@gmail.com with the subject Delete my 599 Scores account.
  2. Send it from the email address you signed in with, or, if you joined the prediction ladder with a name only, tell us the display name you used.
  3. You get a reply confirming it is done. We do not ask you for anything else, and there is nothing to pay.

How long it takes

Your account and the data in the first table are deleted within 30 days of your request, usually the same week. Backups roll over within 90 days, after which no copy remains.

Deleting only part of it

You do not have to delete the whole account. Email the same address and say what you want removed, for example your predictions, or the clubs you follow, and we will remove only that and leave the account in place.

Doing it yourself, on the phone

Signing out in the app removes everything stored on that phone. To remove what is on the server, use the email above.
