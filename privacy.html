599 Scores

Privacy policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

599 Scores is a football app for the Curaçao competitions, run by A3D Printing in Willemstad, Curaçao. This page says exactly what the app stores, why, and how to have it removed. It is short because the app collects very little.

The short version. You can use the whole app without an account and without giving us anything. If you sign in with Google, we keep your name, your email address and your Google account id so your clubs, predictions and notifications follow you to your next phone. We do not sell anything to anyone, and there are no third party advertising or tracking networks in the app.

What we store, and why

  • Nothing, if you just read scores. Fixtures, tables and results are public football data and need no account.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google account id. This is the only way to carry your followed clubs, your predictions and your notification settings across devices.
  • If you join the prediction ladder with a name only: that display name and your predictions. No email, no password.
  • If you turn on notifications: the push address your browser or phone gives us, plus which matches and clubs you asked to be told about. Turning notifications off deletes it.
  • Counting how the app is used: a random number generated on your phone, which screens get opened and which adverts are seen or tapped. It is not tied to your name or your email, and it tells us nothing about you as a person. Advertisers are given totals only, never anything about individuals.
  • If you are a referee with an account: your name as the federation spells it, your phone number if you gave it, the matches you closed and the points you earned.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use third party advertising networks, and there is no advertising SDK in the app. Adverts are sold directly to local companies and are served as plain images by us.
  • We do not ask for your location, your contacts, your photos, your microphone or your camera.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Where it is kept

On Cloudflare servers, in a database that only the app's own server can read. Sign-in is handled by Google, so we never see or store a password. Traffic to and from the app is encrypted.

Children

The app is a football scores app for a general audience. It is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Deleting your data

Signing out leaves nothing behind on that phone. To have your account and everything attached to it deleted from the server, email asccur@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, or write to us at asccur@gmail.com with the display name you used. It is deleted within 30 days, and we will reply to tell you it is done. You can also ask for a copy of what we hold about you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything material will be said inside the app.
