Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 September 2026

599 Scores has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no server of ours to send it to.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about you. We cannot identify you and we do not try to.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few settings in your browser's local storage so it remembers your preferences between visits. This never leaves your phone or computer, and we cannot read it:

  • Your favourite club and the clubs and players you follow
  • Your language choice, Papiamentu or English
  • Your light or dark theme choice
  • Your player of the match votes and score predictions
  • A cached copy of the competition data so the app works offline

Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling it, deletes all of this permanently. There is also a reset button in the Fan Zone.

Network requests

The app makes two kinds of request:

  • Competition data from our own hosting at a3dprintingcw-cyber.github.io, to fetch the latest scores, tables and statistics. No information about you is included in this request.
  • Fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, Google can see your IP address when this happens. Google's handling of that is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. The app works without it, just with different lettering.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Where the football data comes from

Match results, tables, statistics, club badges, fixtures and referee appointments come from the public pages of the Federashon Futbòl Kòrsou (FFK). 599 Scores is an independent app and is not operated by, endorsed by or affiliated with the FFK.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Substantial changes will be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy: asccur@gmail.com

Polítika di Privasidat

Último aktualisashon 19 di sèptèmber 2026

599 Scores no tin kuenta di usuario, no tin reklame i no tin analítika. Nada ku bo ta hasi den e app no ta bai pa un servidor di nos, pasobra no tin un.

Kiko nos ta kolektá

Nada. Nos no ta kolektá, warda, bende ni kompartí informashon personal di bo. Nos no por identifiká bo i nos no ta purba.

Kiko ta keda riba bo aparato

E app ta warda algun konfigurashon den bo browser pa e kòrda bo preferensia. Esaki nunka ta sali for di bo telefon i nos no por lesa esaki:

  • Bo klub faborito i e klubnan i hungadónan ku bo ta sigui
  • Bo idioma, Papiamentu of Ingles
  • Bo tema kla of skur
  • Bo voto pa hungadó di e partido i bo prediksionnan
  • Un kopia di e data pa e app traha sin internet

Si bo kita e app of bòrra su data, tur esaki ta disparsé pa semper. Tin tambe un bòter di reset den Fanzone.

Petishon di réd

  • Data di kompetishon for di nos mes hosting. Ningun informashon tokante bo no ta bai den e petishon aki.
  • Lèter for di Google Fonts. Manera kualke petishon web, Google por mira bo adrès IP ora esaki sosodé.

Di unda e data di futbòl ta bini

Resultado, tabel, statistika, logo di klub, partido i arbiter ta bini for di e páginanan públiko di Federashon Futbòl Kòrsou (FFK). 599 Scores ta un app independiente, no ta operá ni indosá pa FFK.
